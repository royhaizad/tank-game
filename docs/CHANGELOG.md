# Changelog

One-line entries per game mechanic/feature change, newest first. See `CLAUDE.md`
"Keeping Docs in Sync" for when to add to this file.
- 2026-08-31 — Added a small team-colored flag icon (`Menu.drawFlagIcon`) next to every place a team's name is written out as text: the Team Setup heading, the Result screen's "`<team>` Wins!" banner, and the Scoreboard's team rows. Same pole-and-pennant shape as the in-match on-tank flag, just sized for inline text.
- 2026-08-30 — Shield no longer activates the instant it's picked up: it now arms a charge (HUD shows 🛡️(ready)) that only pops into the actual 10s bubble the next time fire is pressed, whether or not that press's shot goes through (Tank.tryActivateShieldCharge, called from tryFire). Added a distinct activation sound (playShieldActivate).
- 2026-08-30 — Fixed a bug where an active shield failed to protect against mine shrapnel. Shrapnel is now absorbed at the shield bubble's edge (like an enemy's laser) rather than passing straight through to hit the wearer.
- 2026-08-30 — Mine shrapnel now sprays in fully random directions instead of 8 evenly-spaced angles with a small jitter.
- 2026-08-29 — Fixed a bug where picking up a Shield crate discarded whatever weapon (and its remaining ammo) was currently equipped, forcing the tank back to the base cannon. Shield is now a pure buff layered on top of the current weapon — Tank.equipWeapon no longer calls revertToCannon() for it.
- 2026-08-29 — Laser and shotgun range increased 20%: laser's bounce cap 5 -> 6 (LaserBeam.MAX_BOUNCES), shotgun pellet lifetime 0.8s -> 0.96s (~160px -> ~192px, Bullet.KINDS.pellet.maxLifetime).
- 2026-08-29 — Mine detonation is no longer instant on trigger: stepping off an armed, revealed mine now lights a 0.5s fuse (Mine.FUSE_DELAY) before it actually explodes, rather than detonating the same frame. The mine stays visible and fully committed once the fuse is lit — nothing can stop it or reset it at that point.
- 2026-08-28 — Weapon rebalance pass. Laser: fires fast rather than instantly — the bounce path locks the moment fire is pressed, but the beam now visibly travels it at 1400 px/s (dodgeable, hit-tested against live tank positions as it goes) instead of resolving on the spot. Missile: slows down once it starts homing (was constant speed the whole flight), now excludes its own shooter from the nearest-target search (previously could re-target and hit whoever fired it), and lasts longer before expiring (6s -> 9s). Mine: trigger radius doubled (6px -> 12px) to match the size of its drawn black body rather than feeling smaller than it looks. Shield: duration extended (6s -> 10s).
- 2026-08-28 — Added a tank-death explosion animation (a flash + expanding fading rings, canvas-drawn placeholder) wherever any tank is destroyed. The match now freezes the battlefield for 2 seconds after the winning kill (only the explosion keeps animating) before switching to the Result screen, instead of cutting away the instant the last tank dies.
- 2026-08-27 — Reworked the laser and mine, replacing their old mechanics entirely. Laser: now bounces off every wall (interior and the outer boundary) with the exact same mirror-angle reflection as the cannon (reuses Maze.moveWithBounce directly), up to 5 bounces, and fires instantly with no charge delay — replacing the old "pierce 1 thin wall, stop at the boundary, 0.5s charge" version. The dotted aim-preview line now traces the real bounce path (aiming aid + telegraph) instead of a straight segment. Mine: no longer kills on contact — stepping on a hidden mine reveals it (with a sound), and stepping back OFF it detonates it into 8 shrapnel pieces sprayed outward (with a sound) that fly straight and do NOT reflect off walls, stopping dead on contact instead. The mine's dropper still gets one free departure before it turns lethal on them too, same grace rule as before, now applied to the new step-off trigger instead of direct contact.
- 2026-08-27 — Fixed a messy stroke/shadow artifact on crate weapon-name labels (most visible on "Mine") caused by too-thick a stroke overlapping itself on tight glyphs — reduced stroke width and added round line joins.
- 2026-08-26 — Shield now deflects ANY bullet off its bubble, including the wearer's own returning ricochet (previously only deflected other tanks' shots; a self-ricochet killed through the shield, per the original section 4 draft). Removes that as a drawback — shield's remaining balance is purely its 6s duration and offering no offense of its own. Unchanged: a mine still kills the wearer on contact, and the wearer's own laser still lands on them (only an enemy's laser gets absorbed).
- 2026-08-26 — Power-up crates now show a readable name label and a distinct procedurally-drawn icon per weapon (three barrels for gatling, a pellet fan for shotgun, a finned dart for missile, a badge for shield, a spiked ball for mine, a lightning bolt for laser) instead of a flat color swatch — same icon reused at HUD size next to each player's weapon status. Added three new synthesized SFX events: a crate appearing, a tank equipping one, and a tank actually firing/using one (a distinct sound per weapon: gatling tick, shotgun blast, missile whoosh, mine thunk, laser charge-sweep — cannon and shield have none, since neither is a "use" event for a power-up).
- 2026-08-25 — Awards now show exactly one name each — a genuine tie suppresses the award for that session instead of listing multiple names — and reveal one at a time, credits-style (fade in, hold, next), on both the Result screen and the Awards modal; click or any key skips ahead.
- 2026-08-25 — Name editing: the field now sits after its tank/team's label instead of above it; a checkmark button inside the field commits like Enter; clicking away from an unsaved change now asks Keep/Discard instead of committing or discarding silently (unchanged edits still close without asking).
- 2026-08-25 — Merged the Medium and Hard AI tiers together: all three tiers (Easy/Medium/Hard) are now selectable on Mission Briefing, none "Coming soon" anymore. Reconciled the two sessions' separate `AI_TIERS` designs into one factory-function table plus a small `AI_AMMO_OVERRIDES` table in src/main.js — Easy and Hard own their ammo limits on their own AI class (per the Hard AI commit); Medium's live in the override table instead (per the Medium AI commit, since MediumAI deliberately doesn't declare its own). See GAME_SPEC.md section 5.1 for the full per-tier axis table.
- 2026-08-25 — Added the Medium AI tier (selectable on Mission Briefing; Hard still "Coming soon" at the time). Medium extends Easy rather than replacing it — same pathfinding and hallway driving — and turns up four dials: a tighter ~9° fire window (vs Easy's ~15°) so it only takes clean shots, 2 bullets in flight + 0.6s cooldown (vs 1 + 1s), a 0.4s re-target cadence (vs 0.8s), and a new dodge that sidesteps bullets on a collision course within 180px. Measured at ~60% win rate vs Easy over 500 headless matches, where an even matchup is 50%.
- 2026-08-25 — Cut predictive/leading aim from the Medium AI after measuring it: with no turret, a shot always leaves along the tank's driving heading, so a lead point only changes *when* the AI fires, never where the bullet goes. It gained nothing against a straight-line target and lost badly against stop-and-pivot ones. Documented in GAME_SPEC.md section 5.1 — aim prediction only works alongside aim-turning, so it belongs in Hard.
- 2026-08-25 — AI tier wiring moved into an `AI_TIERS` table in src/main.js (brain class + per-tier ammo limits in one place); AI ammo is now a per-tier dial instead of a fixed 1-bullet/1s rule for all tiers.
- 2026-08-24 — Custom names: every tank slot and both teams can be renamed (8 characters, typed on the canvas — click a name, type, Enter commits, Escape cancels, empty restores the default). Names are display-only, so a renamed tank keeps its stat history and Reset Stats never clears a name.
- 2026-08-24 — "Session Stats" is now the **Scoreboard**: the tank table is ranked by wins (kills, then fewest deaths, break ties), a separate team Win/Kill/Death table sits below it, and Reset Stats asks for confirmation before wiping anything. The full tables moved off the Result screen into the modal, reached by a new Scoreboard button.
- 2026-08-24 — Added session awards (`src/ui/awards.js`): 12 tongue-in-cheek titles including Most Deadly, Victim of the Situation, Pacifist, Team Killer, Own Goal Enthusiast, Sharpshooter and Cannon Fodder, each explaining itself on hover. The Result screen shows the two or three punchiest; the rest live in an Awards modal off the Scoreboard. Self-kills and team-kills are now tracked per tank to feed them.
- 2026-08-24 — Team mode is now complete and playable. Mission Briefing's single "Battle!" is replaced by two buttons — "All vs All Battle" (starts a free-for-all immediately, unchanged) and "Teams Battle", which opens a new Team Setup screen: a Play All vs All / Play Teams radio, and Team 1 / Team 2 boxes you drag labeled tank tokens between (clicking a token swaps its side). In a team match every tank flies a small team-colored flag, AI targets enemies only (friendly fire still ON), the match ends on last-team-standing with a draw if both are wiped at once, and the win credits a 🏆 to every member of the winning team including its casualties.
- 2026-08-24 — Superseded the same day: the Mission Briefing half of team mode: a Match Mode FFA/2-Team toggle (FFA still the default and unchanged), a manual Team A/Team B picker on every configured tank's row, a team roster read-out, and a Battle button that stays disabled until both teams have at least one tank. The match itself still runs free-for-all — the team win condition is not implemented yet (GAME_SPEC.md section 9.2).
- 2026-08-24 — Decided team mode's friendly fire is ON: a bullet destroys any tank it touches, teammates included, exactly as in FFA — teams change AI targeting and the win condition only, never what a bullet does on impact (GAME_SPEC.md section 9.2). Spec-only; not implemented yet.
- 2026-08-24 — Added the Hard AI difficulty tier (`src/ai/hard.js`, selectable on Mission Briefing): scales Easy up on all four ladder axes — 0.15s fire trigger (was instant-on-sight), 3 bullets in flight + 0.3s cooldown (was 1 + 1s), velocity-leading aim plus calculated multi-bounce bank shots that refuse to ricochet into itself, and bullet-dodging plus flanking approach routes. Movement still goes exclusively through Easy's pathfinder (HardAI extends EasyAI) — dodging and aim-hold are extra states in the same state machine, never raw line-of-sight steering. AI ammo limits moved from a hardcoded value in main.js to per-tier values owned by each AI class.
- 2026-08-24 — Added the power-up system: crates spawn on random empty floor cells every 3–7s (live cap rolled 1–4 per match, never under a tank) and driving over one swaps your weapon until its ammo runs out, reverting to the base cannon. All six weapons implemented — Gatling (15 rounds, 0.09s rate, all in flight at once), Shotgun (5-pellet cone, ~2-cell range, 3 shots), Homing Missile (straight 1s then curves at the nearest tank, shooter included), Shield (6s bubble deflecting other tanks' bullets and absorbing lasers, but never your own ricochet), Mine (3, invisible after 1s to everyone including you, kills any tank on contact once you've driven clear of it), Laser (pierces 1 thin wall, stopped by the outer boundary, with an always-visible dotted aim line and a locked-in 0.5s charge). Spec numbers revised from the original section 4 draft: crates 8–15s/max 1–2 → 3–7s/max 1–4, missile straight time 2s → 1s, and mines now kill any tank rather than only the other one.
- 2026-08-24 — HUD now shows each player's actual equipped weapon (color-swatch icon placeholder + name) with shots remaining, or bullets-in-flight for the base cannon, plus a 🛡️ countdown while a shield is up.
- 2026-08-24 — Hold-to-auto-fire pacing is now per-weapon rather than a fixed 0.5s (gatling repeats every 0.09s; one-shot weapons don't auto-fire at all), and a bullet is now consumed by the tank it destroys so it can no longer kill a second tank in the same collision pass.
- 2026-08-23 — Fixed Easy AI zigzagging and clipping walls: it now compresses its pathfound route into long straight corridor runs (one committed heading per corridor instead of re-aiming every 80px cell) and executes real turns as a deliberate stop-and-pivot at corners instead of swinging through them while still moving. Waypoint/pathing updates now happen every frame instead of on the ~0.8s reaction cadence (only which-opponent-to-target stays on that cadence), so a corner or newly-unreachable route is noticed instantly.
- 2026-08-23 — Fixed the actual root cause of Easy AI getting stuck: it could stay committed to a pathfound waypoint that was no longer reachable at all from its current cell (a wall directly in between) after getting shoved off-course by reversing/collision, and would steer straight at that wall indefinitely. It now re-plans the instant its committed waypoint stops being a direct open grid-step from wherever it actually is.
- 2026-08-23 — Easy AI tuning: fires instantly on aim+line-of-sight (was a 0.5s hold); obstacle recovery now turns to align parallel with whichever wall it hit (picking whichever of the wall's two parallel headings is closer to the waypoint) instead of a fixed-duration blind turn, and forces a fresh path replan (with a flipped turn tie-break) after repeated blocked/reverse cycles so it can't loop forever against a pocket.
- 2026-08-23 — Session stats scoreboard (Result screen + new Mission Briefing "Session Stats" modal, shown only once stats exist) now reorders columns to Win/Kill/Death with an icon + readable word per column, colored by stat type (green/red/white) instead of by tank; the compact in-match HUD stays icon + number only.
- 2026-08-23 — Added in-session Kill/Death/Win tallies per tank slot (HUD shows per-player K/D, Result screen shows a full scoreboard with a Reset Stats button); survives Rematch/Change Difficulty/Back to Title, clears on refresh or Reset — not a persistent ranking system.
- 2026-08-23 — Bullet tuning: slowed bullets from 320 px/s to 160 px/s (just faster than tank top speed, easier to react to); player base cannon now auto-fires every 0.5s while the fire key is held, still capped at 5 in flight (previously one shot per press only, no cooldown).
- 2026-08-23 — Merged all feature branches into main: main is now current and playable (movement, bullets, maze, Easy AI, menus, local multiplayer).
